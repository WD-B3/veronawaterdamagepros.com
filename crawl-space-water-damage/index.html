Crawl space water damage is the standing water, wet insulation, damp soil and moisture in floor framing under a home. If water is in your Verona crawl space, call the 24/7 line. Moisture under a house rises into the floor above, and wet framing can stay wet for weeks if nobody dries it.

This page covers crawl spaces. A full basement has its own page because the access, the drying and the finishes are different.

When a wet crawl space is urgent

Standing water, sagging insulation and a musty smell coming up through floor vents are the signs. Water against ductwork, wiring or a furnace is more urgent. Do not go into a flooded crawl space with live wiring, and call the utility if you are unsure.

If you see water under the house

  • Do not enter if water touches wiring, a panel or a furnace.
  • Turn off the main water if a line is leaking.
  • Check gutters and downspouts for water dumping near the foundation.
  • Photograph what you can from the access hatch.
  • Call the 24/7 line for pumping and drying.

Dispatch is answered nights and weekends. A crew can be sent the same day when one is available.

Where crawl space water comes from

Heavy rain and runoff

Water flows toward the foundation and seeps in at the footing or vents.

High groundwater

A rising water table pushes water up through soil.

Plumbing leaks

A supply or drain line above leaks into the space.

Poor drainage

Downspouts and grading send water to the foundation.

Wet insulation holds water against the subfloor and sags, which keeps the wood wet. Wood framing, subfloor sheathing and ductwork are exposed. Over time, high humidity under the house can support mold and rot in untreated lumber.

The EPA recommends drying wet materials within 24 to 48 hours to hold off mold. A crawl space is harder to dry than a room, so starting early matters.

How crawl space cleanup is done

Step 01

Pump the water

Standing water is removed with pumps and extractors. See water extraction.

Step 02

Remove wet insulation

Saturated insulation is taken out so the framing can dry.

Step 03

Dry the space

Dehumidifiers and air movers run in the crawl space, with readings to confirm the wood is dry. See structural drying.

Step 04

Fix the cause

A drainage fix, a sump or a vapor barrier is handled by the right trade after drying.

Dane County rain and crawl spaces

The August 20 and 21, 2018 floods dropped 11 to 15 inches of rain in parts of western Dane County and showed how quickly water table and runoff can rise. Madison’s 1991 to 2020 normals show 37.13 inches of precipitation a year. Check the FEMA flood map for your parcel, and ask the City of Verona building department about local drainage rules.

What affects crawl space cleanup cost

  • The depth and volume of water.
  • Access, since tight crawl spaces slow work.
  • How much insulation was wet.
  • Drying days.
  • Repair or drainage work after drying.

Insurance depends on the source. Sudden plumbing leaks are often covered, while groundwater seepage often is not. Ask your insurer. Want your real number? Call (608) 871-6605.

Vents, hatches and the ground cover matter too. A torn or missing vapor barrier lets ground moisture rise, and a hatch that does not seal lets outside air carry humidity under the floor. Look at both when the space is dry.

According to the EPA’s moisture guidelines, controlling moisture is what controls mold, which is why a crawl space is dried and kept dry and not only pumped out.

Can wet insulation be saved?

Usually not. Saturated insulation is removed so the framing can dry.

Why does my house smell musty with no visible water?

Damp soil or wet framing under the house can send humid air upstairs. A moisture check finds where.
