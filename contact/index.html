How to reach dispatch

The fastest way to reach us is the phone. Call the 24/7 line at (608) 871-6605 and describe what is happening. Have the address ready, and say whether the water is still running, whether power is on in the wet area and whether you smell sewage or gas.

This site has no contact form on purpose. Water damage gets worse by the hour, and a form can sit unread overnight. A call gets an answer.

Before you call

Have these ready

  • The address of the damage and a call-back number.
  • Where the water came from, if you know, and whether it is still flowing.
  • Which rooms and materials are wet, such as carpet, hardwood, drywall or a ceiling.
  • Whether the water could be sewage or floodwater, and whether any electrical equipment is wet.
  • Photos you have already taken. They help your insurer too.

Find the office

The office is at 503 West Verona Avenue in Verona. The map below is centered on that address. Crews are dispatched from across Dane County, so please call before you travel anywhere.
