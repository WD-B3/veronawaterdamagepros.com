Hardwood floor water damage repair is the drying and, when needed, replacement of wood flooring that has absorbed water. If your Verona hardwood has started to cup, lift or gap, call the 24/7 line. Wood is a natural material that swells with moisture, and what you see on top is only part of what the water did.

Solid hardwood, engineered wood and laminate all respond differently. Laminate fails faster, solid wood can often be dried, and engineered wood depends on its layers.

What cupping, crowning and buckling mean

Cupping

The edges of each board rise higher than the center, because the bottom face took on more moisture.

Crowning

The center of each board rises, which often shows after a cupped floor is sanded before it dried fully.

Buckling

Boards lift off the subfloor in a hump. It usually means the wood took on a lot of water.

Gapping

After drying, boards can shrink and leave gaps wider than before the water.

These are signals, not a diagnosis. A moisture meter tells you what the wood and subfloor actually hold.

When a wet floor is an emergency

Standing water on a wood floor is urgent, because wood absorbs quickly. Stop the source, remove standing water and lift anything that can stain the floor. Do not turn on a heater to dry it fast, because rapid drying can crack the boards.

Right after the water

  • Mop or extract standing water and wipe the surface dry.
  • Move rugs and furniture so air reaches the whole floor.
  • Run a fan across the floor and a dehumidifier in the room.
  • Do not seal the wet floor with polyurethane or cover it with a rug.
  • Photograph the floor and note the time of the leak.

Drying hardwood is slow on purpose. Fast heat can split the boards, while controlled dehumidification pulls moisture out gradually. Crews may use floor-drying systems, which push dry air under or through the boards, along with dehumidifiers and moisture readings.

Boards that have cupped slightly may flatten after drying. Severe buckling usually means replacement of those boards and a check of the subfloor.

Step 01

Measure the floor

Moisture readings of the boards and subfloor set the drying goal.

Step 02

Dry slowly

Dehumidifiers and air movement lower moisture without cracking the wood.

Step 03

Wait and recheck

Boards can keep moving for days, so repair decisions come after readings are steady.

Step 04

Repair or replace

Sanding and refinishing handle mild cupping, and damaged boards are replaced.

Hardwood is sometimes the second casualty of another leak, such as a kitchen leak or an appliance failure. Fixing the source comes first.

Verona winters and wood floors

Madison’s January mean of 19.4°F means heated, dry air indoors in winter and swelling in humid summers. Wood floors move with the seasons, which can be mistaken for water damage. Moisture readings separate normal movement from a leak, and cupping that starts after a known leak is a different case.

What affects hardwood repair cost

  • Species and thickness, and whether it is solid or engineered.
  • Area affected and the number of boards to replace.
  • Subfloor condition.
  • Drying time and equipment.
  • Sanding and finish matching.

Do not try to speed things up with a heat gun or space heater aimed at the boards. Fast drying splits wood and can make cupping worse. Slow, controlled drying with measured readings is what brings a floor back.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard that professionals use for water damage restoration. It is fair to ask any company how its drying of wood floors is measured and logged.

What to do before a crew arrives

  • Do not use a heat gun or space heater on the boards.
  • Do not seal wet wood with polish or finish.
  • Lift rugs and furniture off the wet floor.
  • Wipe standing water from the surface.

Hardwood floor questions

Will my cupped floor go back to normal?

Often it flattens as it dries, especially for clean water. Wait until moisture readings are steady before sanding.

Can I sand the floor right away?

No. Sanding before the wood is fully dry can lead to crowning later.

Is laminate repairable?

Laminate swells and delaminates easily, and it is usually replaced once it has soaked.

Do you also fix the cause of the leak?

A plumber or contractor fixes the source. We dry and repair the damage.
