Ceiling water damage repair is the work of stopping a leak above a ceiling, drying what got wet and replacing the drywall or plaster that cannot be saved. If a ceiling in your Verona home is stained, bulging or dripping, call the 24/7 line now. A ceiling that holds water can fail without warning, so the first minutes are about safety.

Ceiling damage is the symptom. The source can be a roof, an attic, an upstairs bathroom, a supply line or an HVAC drain, and this page covers all of them.

When a wet ceiling is an emergency

A ceiling is an emergency when it bulges, sags or drips, because trapped water adds weight to drywall. Move people and furniture out from under it. If water is dripping near a light fixture or a ceiling fan, keep the switch off and call the utility or 911 about electrical risk before calling us.

If the ceiling is bulging

  • Place a bucket under any drip and move furniture out of the area.
  • Stay out from under the bulge, and do not push on it.
  • Switch off lights and fans on that circuit if the fixture is wet and the panel is dry and reachable.
  • Shut off water to the upstairs fixture or the main valve if a pipe is the source.
  • Photograph the stain from several angles before anything changes.

We answer after-hours calls the same way as daytime ones, and same-day response is available when crews are free. Dispatch gives an honest timing estimate and never promises an arrival time.

Where the water came from

Roof leak

Shingles, flashing and vents let water through, and it soaks insulation before the stain shows below.

Upstairs plumbing

A tub, toilet or supply line leaks into the floor and drips through the ceiling beneath it.

HVAC condensate

A clogged drain line or full pan overflows into the ceiling, which is covered on the AC leak page.

Attic ice and condensation

Warm air meets a cold roof deck, and meltwater or moisture runs back into the ceiling.

A roof leak does not have its own page here because the damage is the same ceiling problem. In a Wisconsin winter, snow cover, ice and thaw cycles drive many leaks. Madison’s 1991 to 2020 normals show 51.8 inches of snow a year and a January mean of 19.4°F, and that pattern means roofs and attics take repeated freeze and thaw.

The National Weather Service rated the June 17, 2014 tornado near Country View Elementary as an EF-3, and wind events like that open roofs. A roofer repairs the opening. We handle the wet ceiling, insulation and framing below it, and we will say so plainly if the roof needs a roofer first.

How ceiling repair is done

Step 01

Find and stop the source

The leak must stop before drying matters. A roofer or plumber handles the repair, and a crew limits the damage meanwhile.

Step 02

Remove what is wet

Saturated drywall and wet insulation come out, since they hold water against framing and invite mold.

Step 03

Dry the cavity

Moisture meters and thermal imaging check joists and attic framing, then air movers and dehumidifiers dry them.

Step 04

Rebuild the ceiling

New drywall, texture and paint match the room. See wall and drywall repair for the patching side.

Mold needs moisture to grow, and the EPA’s flood cleanup guidance advises drying wet materials within 24 to 48 hours. A stained ceiling that stays damp for a week is a mold risk even if the drip stopped.

What affects ceiling repair cost

  • The size of the wet area and how much drywall and insulation is affected.
  • The height and type of ceiling, such as flat, textured or vaulted.
  • Whether framing and the attic need drying.
  • The source repair, which a roofer or plumber quotes separately.
  • The paint and finish work needed to match the room.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for professional water damage restoration. Before a wet ceiling is opened, you can ask any company whether its crews work to it and how they record their readings.

What to do before a crew arrives

  • Stay out from under a sagging or bulging ceiling.
  • Turn off a fixture under a wet ceiling at the switch only if the switch is dry.
  • Move furniture and valuables out from under the stain.
  • Photograph the ceiling before anything changes.

Ceiling water damage questions

Do you handle ceiling water emergencies at night or on weekends in Verona?

Yes. The 24/7 line answers all hours, and a crew can be sent the same day when one is available. A bulging ceiling is treated as urgent.

Is a brown stain on my ceiling dangerous?

A stain means water reached the drywall. It may be old or active. If it grows, feels damp or bulges, treat it as a current leak and call.

Can you fix the roof?

Roof repair belongs to a roofer. We handle the wet ceiling, insulation and framing, and we coordinate with the repair so drying can begin.

Can I just paint over a ceiling stain?

Not until the leak is fixed and the area is dry. Paint over damp drywall traps moisture, and the stain usually bleeds through.
