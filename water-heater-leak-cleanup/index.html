Water heater leak cleanup is the removal of water and drying of the surrounding area after a tank, valve or connection fails. If a water heater in your Verona home is leaking or has flooded the floor, call the 24/7 line. A standard tank can hold dozens of gallons, and when it fails it can flood a utility room or basement fast.

A plumber repairs or replaces the heater. We handle the water, the drying and the damage to what it touched.

When a leaking water heater is an emergency

A heater that is leaking from the tank body, or one that is dumping water from the relief valve, is urgent. Shut off the cold water supply to the tank, turn off the power or gas, and open a faucet to drain it. If the heater is gas and you smell gas, leave and call the gas utility or 911.

Shut it down safely

  • Close the cold water supply valve on top of the tank.
  • Turn off electric power at the breaker, or the gas control valve to off.
  • If you smell gas, leave the house and call the utility or 911.
  • Connect a hose to the drain valve only if the water is cool enough.
  • Photograph the unit, label and leak before it is moved.

Why water heaters leak

Tank corrosion

The steel tank rusts through at the bottom, and the leak is sudden and large.

Relief valve discharge

Excess pressure or temperature opens the valve, which can run for hours.

Loose fittings

Inlet and outlet connections drip and soak the floor slowly.

Sediment and age

Old tanks accumulate sediment and are more likely to fail.

A water heater is often in a basement, a utility room or a closet. Water spreads across a slab quickly, but if it sits on a finished floor it can soak flooring, baseboards and adjoining rooms. If the room has a floor drain, much of the water leaves, but it can still wet walls and wicking materials.

Hot water carries its own risk. Scalding is possible, so do not walk through the water until the heater is off and the water has cooled.

How the cleanup is done

Step 01

Make it safe

Power and water are off, and the area is checked for electrical and scalding risks.

Step 02

Extract the water

Standing water is removed, and floors and low walls are checked with moisture meters.

Step 03

Dry the space

Dehumidifiers and air movers dry flooring and wall bottoms. See structural drying.

Step 04

Restore the area

Baseboards, drywall and flooring are repaired, and a plumber installs the new unit.

If the heater is in a basement, the basement page adds detail on slab drying and finished walls.

Know the age of your heater

Look at the label on the tank. A heater that is many years old and showing rust at the base or fittings is a risk. A drain pan with a line to a floor drain limits damage when the tank fails.

What affects water heater cleanup cost

  • The volume of water and the floor type.
  • Whether it reached finished rooms.
  • Drying days.
  • Repair scope after drying.
  • Whether the heater location holds other equipment.
