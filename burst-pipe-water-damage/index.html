Burst pipe water damage is the flooding, soaking and structural moisture that follow a pressurized supply line failure. If a pipe has burst in your Verona home right now, close the main water valve and then call the 24/7 line. A supply line can release gallons a minute, so every minute before the valve is closed adds to the damage.

This page is about the source of water, a pressurized line in a wall, ceiling or floor. Frozen pipes, water heaters and slab leaks have their own pages because the shutoff and the damage patterns differ.

Burst pipe: what to do first

Find the main water shutoff, usually where the water line enters the house near the meter, and turn it fully off. Then open a faucet on the lowest floor to drain the lines. If water is near the electrical panel or outlets, stay out and call the utility or 911 before you call a restoration line.

The first ten minutes

  • Close the main water valve and open the lowest faucet to drain pressure.
  • Turn off the water heater to protect its elements if the tank is draining.
  • Turn off power only to wet areas, and only if the panel is dry and easy to reach.
  • Move valuables, rugs and furniture away from the water.
  • Photograph the pipe and the water before cleanup begins.

Our 24/7 line is answered nights and weekends, when a lot of pipes seem to fail. Same-day crews are sent when one is available, and no arrival time is promised. A plumber fixes the pipe, and we handle the water.

Why pressurized lines do so much damage

A burst supply line is under pressure, which makes it faster than a slow leak. Water sprays into wall cavities, saturates insulation and drops into floors below. A failure in an upstairs wall can reach three levels, and the stain on the ceiling is often the last sign.

The water is usually Category 1, clean water from the supply, which is the best case for saving materials. It becomes harder to deal with if it sits, because it picks up contaminants as it soaks through building materials.

Failed fittings

Joints, couplings and compression connections let go under pressure.

Corroded pipe

Older metal lines pit and split at weak spots.

Pressure spikes

A sudden surge stresses joints and old pipe.

Impact or work damage

A nail, screw or renovation cut punctures the line.

Verona and burst pipes

Pipes burst in every season, not only in winter, but cold weather raises the odds. Madison’s 1991 to 2020 normals show a January mean of 19.4°F, so exposed pipes in garages and exterior walls are at risk. If the burst was caused by ice, the frozen pipe page covers the thaw and what follows.

Know where your main shutoff is before you need it. Test it once a year to confirm it turns. Many homeowners learn on the day of a burst pipe that the valve is stuck.

What affects burst pipe cleanup cost

  • How many minutes or hours the pipe ran.
  • How many rooms and floors the water reached.
  • Materials affected, such as drywall, insulation, flooring and cabinets.
  • Drying days and equipment.
  • Repair scope after drying.

Burst pipe questions

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Verona?

Yes. Calls are answered all night and all weekend, and crews go out the same day when one is free. Close the main valve first, then call.

Do you fix the pipe?

No, a plumber repairs the pipe. We remove the water, dry the structure and repair the damage.

Can I use the water again before cleanup?

Once a plumber repairs the line and the main valve is back on, yes. Drying continues in the meantime.

How soon should drying start?

As soon as the water is removed. The EPA’s flood cleanup guidance advises drying wet materials within 24 to 48 hours.
