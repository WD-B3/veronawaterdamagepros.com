Water Damage Restoration

Water damage restoration in Verona, WI, explained

Water damage restoration is the work of stopping a water source, removing standing water, drying the structure and repairing what could not be saved. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published industry standard for professional water damage restoration, and homeowners should ask any company, including ours, whether its crews work to it.

The work splits into two phases. Water mitigation comes first and covers extraction, moisture mapping and drying, because wet drywall, subfloor and insulation start to break down quickly. Water damage repair comes second and covers the rebuild, such as new drywall, paint, flooring and trim.

When water is moving through a house in Verona, the first hour matters more than the first estimate. Shut off the main water valve if a pipe is the source, keep people and pets away from standing water, and cut power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and easy to reach. If water is near outlets, a panel or gas appliances, call the utility or 911 before you call us.

Our 24/7 emergency line is built for that moment. We answer, ask what the water touched and where it came from, and explain what a crew will check on arrival. Same-day response is available when crews are free, and the scope and price are explained before any work starts.

Dane County homes fail in a few repeating ways. Supply lines and appliance hoses let go, ceilings stain from roof and attic leaks, sump pumps stop during storms, and drains back up. Each of those has a different fix, which is why this site has a page for each one.

Our Process

The First 72 Hours After Water Damage

Step 01

Call the 24/7 line

Describe where the water came from, what it has touched and whether power or sewage is involved. Safety questions come first, and the crew plan follows.

Step 02

Stop the source

Closing the main water valve ends a supply-line leak. For roof, storm or sewer sources, the aim is to limit spread until the cause can be repaired.

Step 03

Extract and map

Pumps and extraction units remove standing water. Moisture meters and thermal imaging then show where water traveled behind finishes.

Step 04

Dry the structure

Air movers and dehumidifiers pull moisture out of framing, subfloor and drywall. Readings are repeated until materials reach a dry standard.

Step 05

Repair and rebuild

Once the structure is dry, damaged drywall, flooring and trim are replaced, and the scope is matched to what your insurer reviews.

Mold needs moisture to grow, and the EPA’s flood cleanup guidance advises drying wet materials within 24 to 48 hours. That is the reason the extraction and drying steps come before any cosmetic repair. Read more about the drying stage on the structural drying and dehumidification page.

Know Before You Call

How Water Category and Scope Shape the Job

Every water loss is sorted by the cleanliness of the water, because that decides what can be dried in place and what must be removed. Three categories are used across the industry.

CategoryTypical sourceWhat it usually means
CATEGORY 1Supply line, tub overflow, clean rainwaterMost materials can be dried in place if the loss is caught quickly.
CATEGORY 2Washing machine or dishwasher discharge, toilet overflow with urineCarpet pad and porous materials are often removed, and surfaces are cleaned.
CATEGORY 3Sewage, floodwater, long-standing waterPorous materials are removed and the area is treated as contaminated.

Water that sits long enough can move up a category. That is one more reason to call early.

What changes the scope of a water damage repair

Price comes from the work, not from a flat rate. We do not quote a number until someone knows how far the water traveled, and we explain the scope before work begins.

Water category

Clean, gray or contaminated water changes what can be saved and what protective steps are needed.

Affected area

More wet square footage means more equipment, more drying days and more material to replace.

Materials involved

Hardwood, carpet and pad, drywall and insulation each dry at different rates and fail at different points.

Drying time

A cold, damp week in Wisconsin dries slower than a warm one, so equipment stays longer.

Rebuild scope

Painting a patched wall costs differently than replacing cabinets and flooring.

Hidden damage

Water often travels into wall cavities and under floors, which is why readings come before decisions.

Want your real number? Call the 24/7 line and describe the loss. Photos of the wet area help, and so does knowing how long the water has been there.

What Insurance Usually Covers

Homeowners policies often cover sudden and accidental water damage, such as a burst supply line. Gradual leaks and neglect are often excluded, and flood damage usually needs separate flood coverage. The Wisconsin Office of the Commissioner of Insurance also notes that sewer backup and sump pump overflow need a special endorsement. Check your policy or ask your insurer before you assume a loss is covered, and keep photos of everything.

Verona sits on Badger Mill Creek and the Sugar River valley, and the Ice Age Trail and the Sugar River State Trail both pass through the city. Flooding is the other lesson. Dane County reports that 11 to 15 inches of rain fell on parts of western Dane County on August 20 and 21, 2018, and that the rain overloaded the drainage systems built to carry it.

Madison’s 1991 to 2020 climate normals, a nearby long record, show 37.13 inches of precipitation and 51.8 inches of snow in a normal year. A Verona house lives under the same regional pattern of freeze, thaw and rain, and that pattern is hard on pipes, roofs and basements.

Homes close to Badger Mill Creek or other low ground are the ones to check first, and the basement water damage page explains what to do before a sump pump quits. Older homes and newer subdivisions both fail, just in different places, because pipes, roofs and drains age differently.

Common Questions

Water damage restoration FAQs for Verona

Quick answers

Do you handle water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Verona?

Yes. The dispatch line is answered 24 hours a day, including nights, weekends and holidays. Same-day response is available when crews are free, and we cannot promise an arrival time, so we tell you what we know on the call.

What should I do while I wait for a crew?

If a pipe is the source, close the main water valve. Move valuables out of the wet area, keep people and pets out of standing water and turn off power to wet rooms only if the panel is dry and easy to reach. For sewage or electrical risks, stay out and call the utility or 911 first.

Will my homeowners insurance cover it?

It depends on the policy and the cause. Sudden and accidental damage is often covered, while gradual leaks are often excluded and flood usually needs a separate policy. Ask your insurer before work starts, and keep photos and receipts.

How much does water damage restoration cost?

The answer depends on the water category, the affected area, the materials and how long drying takes. We explain the scope and price before work starts. Call and describe the loss for a real number.

Can I dry it myself?

A small clean spill on a hard surface, caught within hours, is often a do-it-yourself job. Water that reached drywall, insulation, carpet pad or the subfloor usually needs moisture readings and equipment. The structural drying page explains the readings and equipment involved.

Is water damage restoration the same as waterproofing?

No. Restoration deals with damage that already happened, while waterproofing changes how water reaches the home. If you need drainage, grading or foundation work, we will say so and point you to the right trade.
