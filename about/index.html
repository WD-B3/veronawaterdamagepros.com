What this business does

Verona Water Damage Pros is a dispatch and coordination service for water damage restoration. When you call, the line is answered by a person who asks where the water came from, what it has touched and whether power or sewage is involved. Those answers decide which licensed and insured crew is sent and what they will check first.

The business address is 503 West Verona Avenue, Verona, WI 53593, and the line runs 24 hours a day, every day. The work includes water extraction, structural drying, repair and rebuild, and the nineteen service pages on this site explain each one in plain language.

How we explain scope and price

Restoration cost comes from the loss, so we do not publish flat prices. Instead, the scope and price are explained before work starts, based on the water category, the affected area, the materials and the drying time. You decide whether to proceed after you hear it.

We say plainly when a job is outside what restoration can fix. A wet basement caused by poor grading needs drainage work, and a failed roof needs a roofer. In those cases we explain the water damage side and point you toward the right trade.

What we do not claim

This site does not carry customer reviews, star ratings or job counts, because we would rather show you how the work is done than ask you to trust a number. We also do not promise insurance approval, and we do not promise an arrival time. We promise to answer, to explain, and to send a crew the same day when one is available.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for water damage restoration, and S520 covers mold remediation. You can ask any company, including ours, whether its crews work to them.

What gets documented

A good job leaves a paper trail. Photos before work starts, moisture readings each day and a written scope are what an insurance adjuster asks for. Ask any company for these, and keep your own copies. The structural drying page explains the readings.

The local conditions that shape the work

Verona is in western Dane County, where freeze and thaw, spring melt and heavy summer rain all put pressure on homes. Madison’s 1991 to 2020 normals show a January mean of 19.4°F, and the National Weather Service recorded an EF-3 tornado near Country View Elementary in Verona on June 17, 2014. We use these facts to explain risk, not to promise outcomes.

Our job is to make the next step clear. If the water is something a mop can handle, we say so. If it is something that needs meters, extraction and drying, we say that too, and explain why. A plain answer on the phone is worth more than a promise.

Keep a short written log of the loss, with dates, times, photos and who you spoke to. It helps with any insurer, contractor or buyer you deal with later.

What this site is for

The pages here are meant to help you decide what to do next. Each service page explains the cause, the first steps, how the cleanup works and what affects cost. The guides in the blog answer common questions about insurance, estimates, records and permits, written for homeowners in Verona and nearby towns.

None of it replaces a conversation about your own water. If something does not match what you see, call and say so. We would rather correct a page than have you follow advice that does not fit your house.

Where we work

The primary service area is the City of Verona in Dane County. Nearby, we cover Madison, Cross Plains, Mount Horeb and Oregon, and the full list is on the service area page. For help right now, the contact page has the number and the map.
