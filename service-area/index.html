Dedicated town pages

The City of Verona is the home base, with the office at 503 West Verona Avenue and the primary ZIP code 53593. Four nearby towns have their own pages because each has different roads, ZIP codes and water risks.

Madison

The largest nearby city, with ZIP codes 53703, 53704, 53705 and 53706 and a lake-and-isthmus geography. See water damage restoration in Madison.

Cross Plains

A village on US 14 in western Dane County, with ZIP code 53528 and a creek flood history. See Cross Plains.

Mount Horeb

A village on US 151 in the Driftless Area, with ZIP code 53572 and rolling terrain. See Mount Horeb.

Oregon

A village where US 14 meets Wisconsin 138, with ZIP code 53575 and an older downtown. See Oregon.

How coverage is decided

Coverage depends on where crews are working and how far they have to travel. Dispatch does not promise an arrival time, and it will tell you honestly if a crew is stretched. When you call, give the street address and the nearest cross street. That lets dispatch judge the distance and the route.

Water emergencies cluster after big storms. If a storm has hit the whole county, expect more calls and tell dispatch as much as you can about the damage so the most urgent jobs go first.

What the weather and terrain mean for each area

Western Dane County has a mix of lakes, creeks and hills. On August 20 and 21, 2018, 11 to 15 inches of rain fell on parts of western Dane County and flooded areas around Black Earth Creek. According to the National Weather Service, an EF-3 tornado struck near Country View Elementary in Verona on June 17, 2014. Madison’s 1991 to 2020 normals show 37.13 inches of precipitation and 51.8 inches of snow in a year.

Each town page explains how its own roads and ground affect water. Cross Plains has the creek, Mount Horeb has the hills, Oregon has an older downtown, and Madison has the isthmus and the lakes.

Homes, rentals and businesses

Dispatch takes calls for houses, apartments, rentals and commercial spaces. If you rent, tell dispatch who owns or manages the building, since the owner may need to approve the work. If you manage a shop, office or multi-unit building, say how many units or tenants are affected and whether you need to stay open.

Water that crosses a shared wall or ceiling is common in older buildings and apartments. The more you can tell dispatch about where it started and where it went, the faster a crew can plan.

If your town is not listed on this page, do not assume it is out of reach. Call, give the address and ask. Dispatch can tell you whether a crew is available for your location that day, and it will say so plainly if the answer is no.

You can also use the map at the bottom of every page to see the area around Verona and judge the distance for yourself.

When to call and when to wait

Call right away for active water, sewage, water near the electrical panel or a wet ceiling that is sagging. For a slow stain that has not changed in weeks, you can call during the day and describe it. Dispatch will say whether it is urgent.
