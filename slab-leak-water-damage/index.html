Slab leak water damage is the wet flooring, baseboards and subfloor that follow a leak in a water line under or inside a concrete slab. In Verona, many houses have basements instead of slabs, so slab leaks are less common than in other regions, and they happen when a home or addition sits on a slab. If you suspect one, call the 24/7 line.

This page is about the damage. A plumber locates and repairs the line, and many homeowners learn about the leak from a high water bill or a warm spot on the floor.

My floor is warm and my meter keeps moving, what is going on?

The signs are hard to see. Your water meter may keep moving when every fixture is off, and the floor can feel warm over a hot line. You may hear running water in a quiet house, or find damp carpet or lifting flooring in one spot.

These signs are not proof. A plumber uses listening equipment and pressure tests to find the leak, and we handle the drying afterward.

Moving meter

The meter dial turns while all fixtures and appliances are off.

Warm floor spot

A hot water line leaks heat into the slab above it.

Sound of water

A hiss or trickle is heard when the house is quiet.

Wet flooring

Carpet, tile or wood lifts or stains without an obvious source.

When a slab leak is an emergency

A slab leak is an emergency when water is coming up through the floor or the meter is running fast. Shut off the main water valve, which stops the leak while a plumber is arranged. If water is near outlets or appliances, call the utility or 911 about electrical risks.

If you think you have a slab leak

  • Close the main water valve and check whether the meter stops.
  • Move rugs and furniture off wet flooring.
  • Photograph the damp area and the meter reading.
  • Call a plumber for leak detection.
  • Call the 24/7 line for any water that has spread.

After-hours calls are answered, and same-day response is available when a crew is free.

Slab leaks in Verona

Many Verona homes are built over basements or crawl spaces, so a true slab leak is less common here than in slab-on-grade regions. Slabs show up under additions, garages and some newer builds. If your home has a basement, a wet floor is more likely a plumbing or basement issue, and our basement page may fit better.

What affects slab leak cleanup cost

  • How long the leak ran before anyone noticed it.
  • Flooring type, such as carpet, tile or wood.
  • Whether the leak reached walls.
  • Drying days.
  • The plumber’s repair, which is separate.

Slab leak questions

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Verona?

Yes. Nights and weekends are covered, and a crew can be sent the same day when one is free.

Do you find the leak?

A plumber locates and fixes the line. We handle the wet flooring and walls.

Why does my meter move when everything is off?

Water is going somewhere. A slab leak is one possibility, and a running toilet or an outdoor line are others. A plumber can tell which.

Are slab leaks common in Verona?

Less so than in slab-on-grade regions, because many houses have basements. Slabs appear in additions and some newer homes.

Can a slab leak damage the foundation?

Long-term leaks can affect soil and the slab. A structural engineer or plumber can assess that.
