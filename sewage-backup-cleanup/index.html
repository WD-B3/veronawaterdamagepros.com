Sewage backup cleanup is the removal, disposal and sanitizing of wastewater that has come up through a floor drain, toilet, tub or sink. If sewage is coming into your Verona home now, keep people and pets out of the area and call the 24/7 line. Sewage is Category 3 water, which means it can carry bacteria and other contaminants.

This is a different job from clean water cleanup. Porous materials that sewage touches are usually removed, not dried.

Gurgling drains and water coming up: what is happening?

Slow drains, gurgling sounds, and water or odor coming up from a basement floor drain are signs that a drain line or the municipal sewer is blocked. Water takes the easiest way out, and in a basement that is often the lowest drain. If more than one fixture backs up at once, the blockage is probably in the main line.

A plumber clears the blockage and finds the cause. We handle the contaminated water and the cleanup afterward.

Main line blockage

Roots, grease or debris block the line and wastewater comes back up.

Municipal sewer surcharge

Heavy rain can overload the public sewer and push water back to low drains.

Collapsed or offset pipe

An old or shifted line stops flow.

Sump or ejector failure

A pump that stops lets wastewater rise in the pit.

What to do while you wait

Safety first

  • Keep everyone out of the wet area, especially children, pets and anyone with health issues.
  • Do not run water, flush toilets or use the dishwasher or washer, which sends more into the line.
  • Turn off power to the area only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely.
  • Wear boots and gloves if you must go in, and wash well afterward.
  • Photograph the water and the damage before cleanup.

Why Category 3 water is different

The EPA’s guidance on flood cleanup treats water contaminated with sewage as a health risk and advises discarding porous materials that cannot be cleaned. That is why carpet and pad that wicked sewage are removed instead of saved.

Cleaning a hard surface and drying a wall cavity are separate steps. The goal is that nothing contaminated stays behind a finished wall.

What affects sewage cleanup cost

Sewage backup questions

Do you handle sewage backups at night or on weekends in Verona?

Yes. Someone picks up at every hour, and a crew can be sent the same day if one is free.

Why is water coming up my basement floor drain?

A blocked main line or an overloaded public sewer can push water back to the lowest drain. A plumber can find which.

Can I clean up sewage myself?

Small spills on hard surfaces may be manageable with protection. A larger backup or anything that reached carpet or walls needs trained crews.

Does homeowners insurance cover sewer backup?

Often only with a water backup endorsement. Check your policy and ask your agent.
