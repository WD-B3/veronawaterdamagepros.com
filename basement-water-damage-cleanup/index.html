Basement water damage cleanup is the pumping, removal and drying work needed after water enters a below-grade space. If your Verona basement is wet now, call the 24/7 line before you carry anything down the stairs. Water in a basement is more than a floor problem, because it can reach the furnace, the water heater, the panel and everything stored on the floor.

Basements fail from the outside in. Sump failure, seepage through walls and floor joints, floor drain backup and window well overflow each leave a different pattern, and the cause shapes the cleanup.

When a flooded basement is an emergency

A basement is an emergency when water is deepening, when it touches the electrical panel or outlets, or when it could be sewage. Do not enter standing water if power is on in the basement. Call the utility or 911 for electrical and gas risks before you call us.

Before anyone goes downstairs

  • Look from the stairs. If water is near outlets, a panel or appliances, do not go in.
  • Shut off the water main if a pipe is the source.
  • Do not run a sump pump extension cord through wet floor.
  • Keep people and pets out of any water that smells like sewage.
  • Photograph the water level and the damage from the doorway.

Dispatch is answered at night and on weekends, because basements flood after storms in the small hours. A crew can be sent the same day when one is available, and we do not promise an arrival time.

Why basements in Verona take on water

Dane County reports that 11 to 15 inches of rain fell in parts of western Dane County on August 20 and 21, 2018, overloading the storm drainage built to carry it. Even in a normal year, Madison’s climate normals show 37.13 inches of precipitation and 51.8 inches of snow, so basements here see spring melt and summer storms. If your lot is near Badger Mill Creek or low ground, check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address.

A sump pump runs on household power, so a storm that knocks out electricity can stop the pump when it is needed most. A battery backup is a question for a plumber, and ask the City of Verona building department about local discharge rules.

Sump failure

A pump that stops, clogs or loses power lets groundwater rise to the slab.

Wall and floor seepage

Water pushes through cracks and the joint where the wall meets the floor.

Floor drain backup

Municipal or private lines overload and push water up through the drain, which can be Category 3.

Window well overflow

Rain fills a well and spills through the window, soaking carpet and finished walls.

Step 01

Pump and extract

Pumps remove standing water and extraction units pull what is left from carpet, pad and low spots.

Step 02

Remove wet finishes

Wet carpet pad, lower drywall and insulation are cut out when they cannot dry, using flood cuts above the water line.

Step 03

Dry the slab and walls

Dehumidifiers matter most below grade, because concrete holds moisture. See structural drying for the equipment.

Step 04

Rebuild and protect

New drywall, flooring and trim return the space, and a plumber or contractor handles any drainage fix.

Cleanup is not waterproofing. If the cause is grading, a cracked wall or missing drainage, restoration will dry the damage but will not stop the next leak, so we say so honestly and suggest the right trade. A basement that flooded from a surface flood may also fit our flood damage page.

What affects basement cleanup cost

  • Water depth, volume and how long it sat.
  • Category of the water, from clean seepage to sewage backup.
  • Finished area, including carpet, drywall, cabinets and furniture.
  • Drying time, which runs longer in a damp basement.
  • Contents that need moving, cleaning or removal.

Insurance for basements is a common trap. Basement water damage questions

Do you handle basement flooding emergencies at night or on weekends in Verona?

Yes. The 24/7 line is answered all hours, and a crew can be sent the same day when one is available. Dispatch explains what to do while you wait.

Can wet carpet in my basement be saved?

Sometimes. Clean water caught fast can be extracted and the carpet dried. Contaminated water or a long soak usually means removal of the carpet and pad.

Will cleanup stop my basement from flooding again?

No. Restoration repairs damage that already happened. Stopping new water takes drainage, grading or sump work, and we point you to the right trade.

Is basement flooding covered by insurance?

It depends on the policy and the cause. Seepage and flood are often excluded, and sewer backup needs an endorsement. Ask your insurer and keep photos and receipts.
