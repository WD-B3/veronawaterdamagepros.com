Carpet water damage restoration is the extraction, drying and treatment of wet carpet and pad, or their removal when they cannot be saved. If the carpet in your Verona home is squishy, stained or smells damp, call the 24/7 line. How quickly it is dealt with decides whether the carpet lives or goes in the dumpster.

Carpet is made of three layers, the face fiber, the backing and the pad, and each layer responds differently to water.

Is the carpet worth saving?

The honest answer depends on the water category and the time. Clean water caught within a day or two often leaves carpet that can be extracted and dried in place, and the pad is replaced or dried separately. Gray or contaminated water, or any water that sat for days, usually means the carpet and pad are removed.

Carpet over a concrete slab dries differently than carpet over a wood subfloor. Water can stay trapped under the pad on concrete, and wood absorbs and holds it.

Time matters. Clean water that sits long enough can move up a category.

What to do right away

While the carpet is wet

  • Remove furniture and anything with legs that can stain wet carpet.
  • Open windows if the weather allows, and run fans pointed across the floor.
  • Pull up small rugs and hang them to dry.
  • Do not use a household vacuum on wet carpet.
  • Photograph the wet area and note when the water started.

Carpet and mold risk

Mold needs moisture, and wet carpet pad is a good place for it. The EPA’s flood cleanup guidance advises drying wet materials within 24 to 48 hours, and it also notes that porous items that stay wet may need to be thrown out. If you smell mustiness days after a leak, ask a crew to check under the pad.

Health questions about mold belong to a physician, and testing and remediation follow the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard.

Verona homes and carpet

Many Verona homes have carpet in basements and bedrooms, and basements see the most leaks. After a wet spring or a hard storm, check carpet near exterior walls and under windows. Madison’s climate normals show 37.13 inches of precipitation a year, so water at the edge of a room is a common starting point.

Carpet questions

Can wet carpet be dried without removing it?

Often yes for clean water caught early, with the pad handled separately. Contaminated water usually means the material comes out.

Should I pull up the carpet myself?

You can lift a corner to check the pad, but a crew has the tools to do it without damaging the tack strip and subfloor.

Why does my carpet smell musty after drying?

The pad or subfloor may still be damp. Ask a crew to take moisture readings.

Do you replace the carpet too?

Restoration covers extraction and drying, and repair work can include replacing carpet and pad. Scope and price are explained first.
