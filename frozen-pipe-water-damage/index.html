Frozen pipe water damage is the leak, flooding and wet structure that follow when ice splits a pipe and then melts. If a pipe in your Verona home froze overnight and now drips, hissing or sprays, call the 24/7 line. The pipe often does not leak while it is frozen, and the damage begins when the ice thaws.

This page is about the ice itself and the thaw that follows. A generic pressurized failure is on the burst pipe page, and the difference matters for how you respond.

When a thaw turns into an emergency

A frozen pipe is a warning, and a thawing pipe can become a flood. If you suspect a pipe is frozen and the faucet is dry, open it so water can flow as it thaws, and shut off the main valve if you see a crack. Do not use an open flame on the pipe.

If you find a frozen pipe

  • Turn off the main water valve if you see a split or bulge.
  • Open the faucet on that line so water and pressure can leave.
  • Warm the area gently with a hair dryer or a space heater placed safely away from anything flammable.
  • Never use a torch or open flame on a pipe.
  • Call a plumber for the pipe and the 24/7 line for any water already spreading.

We answer in the middle of the night during cold snaps, and same-day response is available when a crew is free. Dispatch does not promise arrival times.

Why pipes freeze and split

Water expands by about 9 percent when it freezes. A pipe full of ice builds pressure between the ice and a closed faucet, and the pipe splits at its weakest point. The split is often a thin tear that stays sealed by ice until the temperature rises.

Pipes at risk include those in exterior walls, unheated garages, crawl spaces, attics and near exterior faucets. A wall full of insulation can keep a pipe colder than a heated room.

Exterior walls

Pipes close to the cold side of insulation freeze first.

Unheated spaces

Garages, crawl spaces and attics drop below freezing quickly.

Cabinets on outside walls

Sink bases hold cold air against supply lines.

Hose bibs

An outdoor faucet can freeze back into the wall.

How frozen pipe damage is cleaned up

Step 01

Stop the water

The main valve is closed and the split pipe is isolated, and a plumber repairs it.

Step 02

Remove the water

Extraction removes standing water. See water extraction.

Step 03

Map and open

Moisture readings and thermal imaging find water in walls and ceilings, which often runs further than expected.

Step 04

Dry and rebuild

Dehumidifiers and air movers dry the structure, then drywall, insulation and paint are repaired.

Prevention before the next cold snap

Insulate pipes in unheated spaces and seal air leaks near them. Open cabinet doors on exterior walls during extreme cold. Let a faucet drip slightly during a hard freeze, and keep the heat on if you travel.

Frozen pipe questions

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Verona?

Yes. The 24/7 line is answered at all hours, including in cold snaps. A crew can be sent the same day when one is available.

Is the pipe leaking while it is frozen?

Often not. The ice can plug a split, and the leak starts when it melts.

Can I thaw it myself?

A hair dryer on a low setting is the safe approach. Never use a flame, and call a plumber if you see a split.

Does insurance cover frozen pipes?

It can, but conditions apply. Ask your insurer about freeze damage and heat requirements.
