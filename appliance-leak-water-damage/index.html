Appliance leak water damage is the wet flooring, cabinets and walls that follow a failed hose, drain or valve on a washer, dishwasher, refrigerator or other appliance. If a washer hose has let go or a dishwasher is leaking in your Verona home, call the 24/7 line. These leaks are common and often quick, and the water runs under cabinets and into the subfloor before anyone notices.

The leak itself is fixed by an appliance or plumbing technician. We handle the water and the damage it caused.

When an appliance leak is an emergency

A running appliance leak is urgent until the water is shut off. Turn off the shutoff valve behind the appliance, or close the main water valve if you cannot reach it. Unplug the appliance only if the cord and outlet are dry.

Stop the leak first

  • Close the shutoff valve behind or under the appliance.
  • If you cannot find it, close the main water valve.
  • Unplug the appliance only if the outlet is dry and you are standing on a dry surface.
  • Pull the appliance away from the wall if it is safe, and mop standing water.
  • Photograph the hose, valve and floor before anything is moved.

We answer after-hours and weekend calls, which is when most washer and dishwasher leaks seem to happen, and a crew can be sent the same day when available. Dispatch does not promise an arrival time.

Where the water comes from

Washing machine

A cracked or loose supply hose, a bad drain hose or an overflowing drain standpipe can release large volumes fast.

Dishwasher

Supply line, door gasket or drain hose failures soak the cabinet base and floor.

Refrigerator

A worn ice maker line or a clogged defrost drain leaks slowly under the unit.

Water softener or other units

Drain and brine lines can overflow into utility rooms and basements.

A washing machine hose can run for hours while a household is out. The water spreads across the floor, moves under baseboards and drops through floor gaps to the room below. A leak that started in a first-floor laundry room can show up as a stain in the basement ceiling.

Dishwasher leaks and refrigerator lines usually spread under cabinets and flooring, where the damage is hidden and the water stays for days.

How the cleanup works

Step 01

Extract and remove

Standing water is extracted and any soaked mats, kick plates or soft materials are removed.

Step 02

Check hidden spaces

Meters and thermal imaging check under the cabinets, behind baseboards and in floors below.

Step 03

Dry with equipment

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the floor system. See structural drying.

Step 04

Repair the damage

Floors, baseboards and cabinet bases are repaired, and cabinets are covered in the kitchen page.

If the leak soaked hardwood, the hardwood floor page explains what happens next.

Prevention that actually helps

Replace rubber supply hoses with braided ones, and check them when you replace the appliance. Do not run a washer or dishwasher while the house is empty overnight. Water leak sensors on the floor behind the unit are inexpensive and give an early alert.

What affects cleanup cost

  • Volume of water and how long it ran.
  • Whether cabinets, flooring and subfloor were affected.
  • Whether the water reached a lower floor.
  • Drying days.
  • Repair scope after drying.

Sudden appliance leaks are often covered by homeowners insurance, while a leak from a hose that failed over time may be treated differently. Check your policy. Want your real number? Call (608) 871-6605.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for professional water damage restoration, and you can ask any company whether its crews work to it when an appliance leak has soaked a floor.

What to do before a crew arrives

  • Unplug the appliance only if the cord and outlet are dry.
  • Close the shutoff valve behind or under the appliance.
  • Do not use the appliance again until it has been checked.
  • Keep children and pets out of the wet area.

Appliance leak questions

Do you handle appliance leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Verona?

Yes. The 24/7 line is answered at all hours, and a crew can be sent the same day when one is available.

Who fixes the washer or dishwasher?

An appliance or plumbing technician fixes the appliance. We dry and repair the damage.

Is a slow refrigerator leak a big deal?

It can be. A slow leak under a refrigerator can rot flooring and soak the subfloor over months.

Should I replace the flooring?

Not always. Readings show whether it can dry in place, and many floors can be saved if the water is caught early.
