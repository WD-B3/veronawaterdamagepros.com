Cross Country Heights is an established residential neighborhood on the north side of Verona. City of Verona park records name two neighborhood parks here. Cross Country Park covers 5 acres and is reached from Basswood Avenue, Aspen Avenue and Linden Street. Behnke Park covers 3.6 acres and is reached from Mahogany Way, Spruce Circle, Hickory Court, Basswood Avenue and Maple Road.

A real estate guide describes most homes as built in the 1980s and 1990s, in ranch, colonial and multi-level styles. If water is spreading through one of them, call and tell dispatch which rooms it has reached.

Park-edge lots and mature trees

The same guide says many lots here sit along the edges of the two parks, and some back onto them. Mature trees line many of those yards.

Trees matter for water in one specific way. In any neighborhood with large trees, roots can work into older sewer pipes and slow a drain, which can lead to a backup. That is a general plumbing fact and not a finding about any street here, and a plumber can check a line with a camera.

Homes that are now decades old

Fixtures installed in the 1980s and 1990s may be reaching the end of their service life. Water heaters, washing machine hoses, toilet fill valves and under-sink supply lines all wear out. This is a general maintenance point and not evidence that homes here have more leaks.

A simple habit helps. Look under each sink and behind the washer twice a year, and replace rubber hoses that are cracked, bulging or stained.

Storm history near Country View Elementary

Real estate guides place Country View Elementary within a short walk of Cross Country Heights. According to the National Weather Service, the tornado of June 17, 2014 near Country View Elementary was an EF-3. The record does not break damage down by neighborhood, so it should not be read as a finding about homes here.

The Dane County hazard plan for Verona also records rain and wind on July 17, 2012 that caused $253,063 in roof and water damage at Country View Elementary. It shows how a roof opening during a storm can let water into a building. The FEMA flood-zone status of this neighborhood has not been verified. Search your own address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, since zones are mapped parcel by parcel.

What to do in the first hour

Step 01

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main water shutoff. If the source is a roof leak, place a bucket and move items away from it.

Step 02

Make the area safe

Keep clear of water near outlets and the panel. Call the utility for sparking or a burning smell.

Step 03

Call and describe

Tell dispatch what the water has touched and how long it has been there.

Step 04

Record the damage

Photograph stains, wet floors and affected items before cleanup begins.

Getting wet carpet and pad dry

Carpet can sometimes be saved if the water was clean and drying starts quickly. The pad underneath is usually replaced, because it holds water and dries slowly. A crew lifts the carpet, extracts the water and dries the subfloor before anything is laid back down.

Water from a sewer line or an outside source is a different case. That carpet is generally discarded.

Documenting a loss for your insurer

Your policy decides what is covered, and the cause of the water matters. Take dated photos of the damage and keep receipts for any emergency costs. An itemized scope from the restoration crew can describe the work, but it does not promise payment.

Safety first

  • Do not enter a flooded basement if water may reach outlets or the panel.
  • Do not use ordinary fans or a household vacuum on sewage or other contaminated water.
  • Avoid disturbing old flooring, plaster or pipe wrap that may contain asbestos.
  • Shut off water only where it is safe and easy to reach.

Cross Country Heights water damage questions

Where is Cross Country Heights?

It is on the north side of Verona. City records place Cross Country Park off Basswood Avenue, Aspen Avenue and Linden Street, and Behnke Park off Mahogany Way and Maple Road.

Are older homes more likely to leak?

Age alone is not proof. Components do wear out, so older hoses, valves and water heaters deserve a regular look.

Can tree roots cause a sewer backup?

They can in any neighborhood with older clay or cast iron lines. A plumber with a camera can confirm the cause before anyone assumes it.

Does the 2014 tornado record mean this area was flooded?

No. It records a tornado near Country View Elementary and does not describe flooding or neighborhood damage.

How long does drying take?

It depends on what was wet and for how long. Many jobs take several days of equipment time, with readings taken before equipment is removed.
