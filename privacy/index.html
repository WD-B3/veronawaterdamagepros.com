Privacy Policy

This site is call-only. It has no forms and does not ask you to type personal details into a page.

What we collect

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

When you call, we ask for your name, address and a call-back number so a crew can reach you. We use that information to dispatch service and to follow up on the job.

This website has no contact form, login or checkout. It may record basic technical data that most web servers log, such as the page visited, the browser type and the approximate time of the visit.

How we use information

Information from a call is used to dispatch a crew, to explain scope and price, and to support an insurance claim if you ask. We do not use it for any other purpose without telling you.

Cookies and third-party services

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and a map from Google Maps. Those services may receive your IP address when the map or fonts load. Please review their own policies for how they handle it.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the call details we hold for you by calling the dispatch line at (608) 871-6605 or emailing contact@veronawaterdamagepros.com. For questions about this policy, use the same number or address.
